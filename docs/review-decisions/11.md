# Review decisions of pull request #11 (vertical 1b: the phone)

## R-11-1 | architecture | major | fixed

- **Finding**: With the home server stopped and the phone online, the proxy answers 502/503/530: the kept page was not served and the session opened as an error, so the photos could not wait.
- **Where**: `web/src/lib/serviceWorker.ts` (`networkFirst`), `web/src/lib/session.ts` (`openSession`).
- **Reason**: One rule, `isUnreachable`: no answer, 429, 502-504 and Cloudflare's 52x/530 mean "unreachable". `networkFirst` serves the kept page then, and `openSession` opens the offline page; unit tests for both, and the `@offline` e2e test now checks the administration with the back end stopped.

## R-11-2 | architecture | major | fixed

- **Finding**: Every other status stopped the outbox as "no network": a photo the back end always fails on blocked the others for ever, under a false message.
- **Where**: `web/src/lib/outbox.ts` (`flush`).
- **Reason**: An error of the back end itself is the state `server`, with its own message; the photo is counted, tried again at the next sending, set aside as `ServerError` after three failures, and never stops the photos behind it (unit test).

## R-11-3 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The manifest and the icons sit at the root, so a build without `admin/` would still publish them.
- **Where**: `web/public/manifest.webmanifest`, `web/public/icons/`.
- **Reason**: Moved to `web/public/admin/`; the manifest's paths are relative (`./`), and leaving `admin/` out leaves the whole installable administration out.

## R-11-4 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The order in which inbox photos are attached is a product rule written in the endpoint.
- **Where**: `src/AdriaMo.Api/Inbox/InboxEndpoints.cs`.
- **Reason**: `InboxPhoto.ObservedAt` in Core (the `Observation` rule), unit-tested; the endpoint orders by it.

## R-11-5 | architecture | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `InboxEndpoints` calls the helpers of `ConstatEndpoints`, made internal for it.
- **Where**: `src/AdriaMo.Api/Constats/ConstatEndpoints.cs`.
- **Reason**: They are the checks and the mapping of a constat request: one place for them is the point; a class of its own for four one-liners adds a file and no rule.

## R-11-6 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: A file that cannot be deleted at discard answers 500 although the discard is done; other save failures leave orphan files.
- **Where**: `InboxEndpoints.DiscardAsync`, `AddAsync`, `PhotoIntake.Forget`.
- **Reason**: `Forget` never throws (it says whether every file went) and the discard logs a warning; only the unique-index race is caught at upload (quality R-11-19), other failures surface. An orphan sweep, if ever needed, comes with the backups (vertical 6).

## R-11-7 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The outbox is per browser, not per account: photos left at logout leave under the next account.
- **Where**: `web/src/lib/outboxStore.ts`, `AdminApp` logout.
- **Reason**: Logging out forgets the waiting photos, after a warning that says how many (spec §4.1); a phone is personal, and a revoked session keeps them for its owner's next login.

## R-11-8 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: Revoked phones and expired codes stay for ever; the list filters them in memory.
- **Where**: `src/AdriaMo.Api/Accounts/DeviceEndpoints.cs`.
- **Reason**: Asking for a code deletes the account's revoked phones and unscanned codes; the list filters in SQL.

## R-11-9 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: With an absolute API address the service worker silently posts to `/api/v1`.
- **Where**: `web/src/lib/serviceWorker.ts` (`apiPathOf`).
- **Reason**: The page does not register the service worker when the API is not a path of the site (warning in the console); the page then sends the photos itself.

## R-11-10 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: Nothing tells a newcomer how to try the phone features against the sandbox.
- **Where**: `deploy/sandbox/README.md`.
- **Reason**: A section "Trying it from a phone": the secure context, the HTTPS proxy, the address in the QR code, `yarn dev`.

## R-11-11 | security | medium | fixed

- **Finding**: Any site can post a form to the share target: the service worker stored and sent the photos under the administrator's session, past both CSRF defences.
- **Where**: `web/src/sw/sw.ts`, `web/src/lib/serviceWorker.ts` (`acceptShare`).
- **Reason**: A shared photo is stored as `shared` and never sent before its owner confirms it on the page ("Envoyer ces N photos", with the thumbnails) or ignores it; at most 50 photos a share and 300 in the outbox. Unit tests (not sent before confirmation, limits) and the e2e test goes through the confirmation. Spec §4.1.

## R-11-12 | security | medium | deferred

- **Finding**: The keys that sign the session cookies are unencrypted in the data volume, which the backup copies: a reader of a backup could forge a session.
- **Where**: `src/AdriaMo.Api/Program.cs`.
- **Reason**: Decided in spec §10.6: the backup leaves `keys/` out (a restore logs everyone out). There is no backup yet: it is built with vertical 6. Encrypting the key ring with another mounted secret protects nothing more against the root of the host, who can read both.

## R-11-13 | security | low | fixed

- **Finding**: A phone session has every power of its account, including pairing another device.
- **Where**: `DeviceEndpoints.CreateAsync`.
- **Reason**: Only a session opened with the password pairs a phone (403 for a phone, tested). A phone keeps the other powers: the super-admin may need to reset a password from it, and removing the phone or changing the password ends it.

## R-11-14 | security | low | fixed

- **Reason**: The code is claimed by one conditional `ExecuteUpdate` (`CodeHash` and `PairedAt` in the condition); a test sends four scans at once and gets one session.
